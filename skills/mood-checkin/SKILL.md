# Mood Check-in — haal poochhna

User ne khud enable kiya ho ("roz mood poochha karo") to ek line poochho "Aaj kaisa
mahsoos kar rahe ho?" — jawab sunke halka empathetic response do, advice mat thopo.
Agar jawab me distress/serious concern lage to reminders-tasks/emergency-contact
skill ke resources approach follow karo, khud diagnose mat karo.
