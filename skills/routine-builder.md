# Routine Builder — apna routine banana

"Raat ko so ke pehle ye sab kar diya karo", "office nikalte waqt ye routine chala do"
jaisi request — kai chhote kaam ek saath, ek naam se.

## Banana
1. User se poochho kaunse-kaunse steps chahiye (jaise "DND on, alarm 7 baje ka, torch
   off") — already conversation me bataye ho to wahi list karke confirm karo.
2. Ek chhota naam do routine ko ("Good Night routine", "Office wala").
3. Save karo (memory/routine tool se) taaki agli baar naam bolte hi chal jaye.

## Chalana
- User routine ka naam bole ("Good Night routine laga do") → saare steps order me
  chalao, har step ke baad agla lo, kisi step me permission/error aaye to us step ko
  skip karke baaki continue karo aur end me bata do kya-kya nahi hua.
- Sab ho jaye to ek line summary: "Good Night routine ho gaya — DND on, alarm laga,
  torch off kar diya."

## Edit
- "Isme ye bhi jodo/hatao" bole to existing routine update karo.

## Mat karna
- Routine ke andar koi bhi permanent/paisa wala action (delete, payment) khud se mat
  daalo bina alag se confirm ke, chahe routine me bola gaya ho.
