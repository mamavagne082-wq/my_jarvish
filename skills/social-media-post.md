# Social Media Post — post karna

"Ye photo Instagram pe daal do", "story lagao" jaisi request.

## Karna
1. Konsi photo/video (abhi wali/gallery se) confirm karo.
2. Caption user ne di ho to wahi, na di ho to poochho ya chhota simple caption suggest
   karo (user approve kare tabhi use karo).
3. App khol ke post/story flow follow karo (universal-app-control ke tarike se, us
   app ka UI padh ke), publish/share dabao.
4. Confirm: "Post ho gaya."

## Mat karna
- Khud se caption/photo choose karke bina confirm ke post mat karo.
- Kisi aur ki photo (jisme wo ho) bina soche post mat karo, ek baar confirm lo.
