# Weather Check — mausam ka haal

"Aaj mausam kaisa hai", "barish hogi kya", "kal ka temperature kya hai" jaisi request.

## Kaise batao
1. Weather tool (agar device me available ho) se current location ka data lo.
2. Ek line me practical summary: "Abhi 32°C hai, dopahar tak barish ka chance hai —
   chhata rakh lena."
3. "Kal ka" poochhe to forecast se batao, clearly bolo "forecast hai, exact nahi hota".

## Practical suggestion
- Barish ka chance zyada ho aur user bahar jaane ka bole to chhata/raincoat suggest
  karo, ek line me — lecture mat do.
- Bahut garam/thanda ho to hydration/warm clothes ka chhota reminder de sakti ho.

## Fail case
- Weather tool available na ho to seedha bata do "mujhe live weather data nahi mil
  raha", guess/purana data mat do jaisa sach ho.
