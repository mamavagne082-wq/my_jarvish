# Calculator Quick — turant hisaab

"15% ka discount kitna hua", "225 divided by 9", "20000 ka 12% kya hai" jaisi request.

## Kaise karo
1. Khud calculate karke seedha answer bolo — calculator app kholne ki zarurat nahi
   simple/medium maths ke liye.
2. Real-life context ho (discount, tip, split bill) to result ko practical tarike se
   batao: "1500 ka 20% discount = 300, to final price 1200 hoga."
3. Bahut complex/lambi calculation (multi-step financial, scientific) ho aur galti ka
   risk ho to calculator tool/app use karke double-check karo.

## Mat karna
- Approx answer ko exact bata ke confuse mat karo — round kiya ho to "lagbhag" bolo.
