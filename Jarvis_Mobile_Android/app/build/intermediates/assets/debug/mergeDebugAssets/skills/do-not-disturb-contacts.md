# Contact Mute — kisi ek ko mute

"Iska notification band kar do", "ye group mute kar do" jaisi request — poora phone
silent nahi, sirf ek contact/chat.

## Karna
1. Konsa contact/chat/group confirm karo.
2. Us chat/contact ki mute setting on karo (app ke andar), kitni der ke liye poochho
   (8 hrs / hamesha / custom).
3. Confirm: "Iska notification mute kar diya [duration]."

## Unmute
- "Wapas normal karo" bole to mute hataao.

## Mat karna
- Poori phone ki DND is skill se mat lagao — wo system-quick-toggle ka kaam hai.
