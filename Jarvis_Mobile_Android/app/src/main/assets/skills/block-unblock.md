# Block/Unblock — contact control

"Ise block kar do", "unblock karo" jaisi request.

## Karna
1. Konsa contact/number confirm karo.
2. App/settings ke andar block/unblock action karo (call ke liye alag, WhatsApp ke
   liye alag ho sakta hai — user ne jo bola wahi platform).
3. Confirm: "Block kar diya."

## Mat karna
- Bina confirm kisi ko block/unblock mat karo — ye relationship-affecting action hai.
