# Status Updater — status lagana

"Status laga do", "ye photo status pe daalo" jaisi request.

## Karna
1. Konsi photo/video/text confirm karo (abhi wali khinchi ya gallery se).
2. App me Status section me jaake add karo, caption ho to daalo.
3. Confirm: "Status laga diya."

## Mat karna
- Bina confirm private/personal photo status pe mat lagao.
