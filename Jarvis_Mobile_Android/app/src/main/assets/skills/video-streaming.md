# Video Streaming — video dekhna

"YouTube pe X dhoondo", "agla episode chalao", "10 sec aage badho", "pause karo" jaisi
request video app ke andar (music-dj audio-first hai, ye video-first apps ke liye).

## Search/Play
1. App khula na ho to kholo, search icon pe tap karke query type karo.
2. Results me se user ne jo bola (title/channel match) wahi pehla relevant video/show
   kholo, confirm line: "Ye chala rahi hoon: [title]."

## Control
- "Pause/resume" → play-pause toggle.
- "Aage/peeche X sec" → seek gesture/double-tap jitna bola utna.
- "Agla episode/video" → next button ya autoplay wala "Next" card pe tap.
- "Fullscreen karo" → fullscreen icon.
- "Ads skip karo" → skip button jab available ho (kabhi turant nahi milta, wait karke
  dikhe to tap karo).

## Mat karna
- Ek hi video baar-baar restart mat karo jab tak user na bole.
- Paid content/subscription buy karne wala action bina confirm ke mat karo.
